DESCRIPTION:
Dugout IQ takes the guesswork out of game day. Built by a real youth coach, it handles the stuff you used to scribble on napkins — batting orders, defensive rotations, and position tracking — so you can focus on your players.

WHAT IT DOES:

Roster Management — Add players with jersey numbers, star ratings, and position preferences. Mark available pitchers and catchers. The app knows who plays where.

Smart Defensive Rotations — Set competitive or development mode per inning. The rotation engine places the right players at the right positions based on ratings, preferences, and fairness. No one rides the bench all game.

Batting Order — Auto-generated based on on-base percentage or points. Drag to reorder. Tracks at-bats with simple tap scoring or advanced mode (1B, 2B, 3B, HR, BB, HBP, SAC).

Live Game Day Scoring — Tap a player, tap the outcome. Real-time at-bat logging with inning tracking and a live scoreboard. Send a scorer link to an assistant in the stands — no login needed.

Parent Portal — Share a read-only link with parents. They see their kid's stats, batting average, on-base percentage, and hot streaks. No login, no app download needed.

Printable Dugout Cards — Generate a pocket card with your batting order, defensive lineup, and bench rotation. Print it or screenshot it. Stick it in your back pocket.

Game History — Every committed game is saved. View position history across all games, season batting stats, and per-player trends.

Multi-Team Support — Coach multiple teams? Switch between them in one tap. Each team has its own roster, stats, and settings.

WHO IT'S FOR:
Rec league coaches, travel ball coaches, and anyone managing a youth baseball or softball team who wants fair play and organized game days.

Free to use with 2 games and 70 at-bats. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited access.
