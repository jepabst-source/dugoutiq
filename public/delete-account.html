Delete Your Account

Last updated: April 19, 2026

You can request deletion of your Dugout IQ account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to make the request, what gets deleted, what is retained, and how long it takes.

How to Request Deletion

Email us from the address associated with your account:

Send an account deletion request to:

support@lineupman.com

Use the subject line: Delete My Account. Send from the email address you use to sign in to Dugout IQ. We use this to verify the request.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your user account (email, display name, profile photo)
  • All teams you created (rosters, player data, ratings, position preferences, attendance)
  • All games and at-bat records associated with your teams
  • All defensive lineups and rotation history
  • Any subscription metadata stored on our servers
  • Your push notification tokens (if push was enabled)

What Is Retained

Some limited data may be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes:

  • Payment records: Stripe and Apple/Google retain transaction records independently per their own retention policies. We retain only the metadata required for tax reporting (purchase date, amount, last 4 digits of payment method).
  • Aggregated analytics: Anonymous, non-identifiable usage statistics that cannot be traced back to your account.
  • Server logs: Standard infrastructure logs are retained for 30 days for security and debugging purposes, then automatically purged.

How Long Deletion Takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them. In most cases, the deletion completes within a few business days. You will receive an email confirmation when the deletion is complete.

If You Are an Assistant Coach

If you were invited to a team as an assistant coach and want to remove your access without deleting the head coach's data, you can either:

  • Ask the head coach to revoke your access from the team's settings, or
  • Email support@lineupman.com and we will remove you from the team while preserving the team and its data.

Cancelling a Subscription Without Deleting Your Account

If you only want to cancel a Dugout IQ Pro subscription (not delete your account):

  • Web subscriptions (Stripe): Email support@lineupman.com and we will cancel and refund per our refund policy.
  • Android subscriptions / one-time purchases: Manage in the Google Play Store app under Subscriptions or Purchase History.
  • iOS subscriptions / one-time purchases: Manage in Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Questions

Email support@lineupman.com for any questions about account deletion.
