Roster

Your whole team, organized

Add players with jersey numbers, defensive ratings, position preferences, and coach notes. Mark who can pitch and catch. See batting averages and OBP at a glance.

Rate each player 1–5 stars for defense. The rotation engine uses these ratings to put the right kids at the right positions in the right innings — so you don't have to figure it out on a napkin.

Edit anything on the fly. Roster changes sync instantly across all your devices.

Fielding

Fair rotations, zero arguments

Our rotation engine generates defensive lineups that respect player skill ratings, enforce bench fairness, and balance playing time automatically. Set competitive or development mode per inning.

No more parents asking "why didn't my kid play shortstop?" — the algorithm handles it, and the data backs it up. Swap any player with a tap, and the rest of the lineup cascades automatically.

Includes pocket cards for late-inning situations: LFG mode puts your best players at key positions, OOR mode rests your starters.

Play Ball

Track at-bats in real time

Tap a player, tap an outcome. Simple mode (K, Hit, Walk, Out) or Advanced mode (1B, 2B, 3B, HR, BB, HBP, SAC). Works during games or practice.

Share a scorer link with anyone in the stands — they can log at-bats from their phone, no account needed. You coach, they track. Stats sync automatically.

OBP, batting average, and rolling form update in real time as at-bats are recorded.

Parent Portal

Keep parents in the loop

Share a link and a 4-digit code. Parents see their kid's OBP, batting trends, recent positions played, and more. Stats update live — no refreshing needed.

When a kid is on a hot streak, parents see 🔥. Only positive trends — no kid gets called out for struggling. It's the kind of thing that makes parents love the coach.

Print

Printable dugout cards

One-tap print: batting order on one side, defensive lineup on the other. Includes pocket cards for late-inning substitutions. Tape it to the dugout wall.

On mobile? Export as an image and print from your phone's photo gallery. Everything formatted clean on white — ready for the dugout.
